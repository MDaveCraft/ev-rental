# **2. Problem**

Despite policy tailwinds and growing awareness, India’s EV adoption is constrained by structural barriers:

* **Upfront EV cost**: ₹10–25 lakhs (vs ₹8–12 lakhs for ICE)
* **Range anxiety**: 40% cite battery fear as primary blocker
* **Infrastructure gap**: only **25,000 chargers** nationwide (vs required 2.9M by 2035)
* **Minimal EV options** on existing rental platforms
* **No integrated charging discovery/payment ecosystem**
* **Low first-time user confidence** → operational errors & poor rental experiences

The market lacks an **intelligent rental aggregator** — one that stitches together supply, charging, education, and predictive trip orchestration.
