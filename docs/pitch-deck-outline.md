## **Slide 2 — The Problem**

India’s EV adoption is growing, but major barriers remain:

* **High EV upfront costs (₹10–25 lakhs)**
* **Range anxiety (40% of potential users)**
* **Charging scarcity** — only **25,000 public chargers** vs **2.9M needed by 2035**
* **Fragmented rental experience** (Zoomcar, Revv → minimal EV options)
* **Lack of EV education** → misuse, anxiety, poor customer experience
* **Closed ecosystems** → no interoperability

**Result:** EV rentals are unreliable, inaccessible, and not scalable.
